Paws Haven Animal Rescue

## Animal Rescue and Care Centre

## The Paws Haven Animal Rescue website is a responsive website designed for a fictional non-profit animal shelter in Cape Town. The website will provide information about the organisation, animals available for adoption, volunteering opportunities, donations, and ways for visitors to get in contact with the shelter. Key features will include an animal adoption section, donation and volunteer information, an image gallery, contact forms, and easy-to-use navigation across the website. The main goals of the project are to increase awareness of Paws Haven, encourage animal adoptions, attract volunteers and donors, and make important information easily accessible to the community. The website will provide a user-friendly and visually welcoming experience while helping the organisation reach a wider audience and support more animals in need.
